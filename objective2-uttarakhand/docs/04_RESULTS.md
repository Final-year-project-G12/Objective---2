# Results - Objective 2, Uttarakhand (current pipeline)

Generated by `scripts/g12_results_doc.py` from the saved files named under each table; do not edit by hand. Every headline value is also a row in `results/results_registry.csv`. Gains are PAIRED differences against a water-only tank at the same flow, weather year, demand, shield and tank model. Energy figures are E_del (energy delivered up to the delivery target) in kWh per year, `g_window` for the 18:00-09:00 window and `g_annual` for the whole year. **The Uttarakhand delivery target is 50 C** (`configs/system_config_shared.yaml`); column names ending in 45 are kept from the code but are computed at 50 C. `d_overheat` is shield-hours of the PCM tank minus the water-only tank.

Chain: the Rajasthan v3 recipe (PCM fraction cap 30 %), 4 Objective 1 regimes (K = 4), 12 (regime, PCM) pairs, explore years 2016-2020, select years 2021-2023, held-out years 2024-2025 used once. All diagnostics (sections 1b, 5b-5e) were run on these designs.
