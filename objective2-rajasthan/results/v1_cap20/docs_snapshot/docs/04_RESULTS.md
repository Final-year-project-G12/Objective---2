Reading: the zero or negative `tau_serve` gain is the net of two opposite effects. The energy delivered up to 45 C in the same scenario is HIGHER with PCM than with the 50 L water-only tank in every regime and select year, and the sensible-only (L = 0) case is lower than the water-only tank, so that energy gain comes from the latent heat. Reading: the inner-radius edge is NOT limiting: at the chosen fraction a larger radius is worse (R 45 mm and R 50 mm below the chosen design in every regime), so 40 mm sits at or near an interior optimum (the sampling is coarse: 30, 40, 45 mm). The cap is administrative (the packing engine accepts 28-48 % at R 40 mm) and its only literature support in the files read is the 10-20 % range of Chen 2025; whether to raise it is a decision for the guide, not taken here.
