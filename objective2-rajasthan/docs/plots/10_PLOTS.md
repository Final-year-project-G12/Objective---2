# Phase figures (Rajasthan, Phases 2-8 equivalents)

Generated by `src/plots/make_plots_phases.py`:

```
python pipeline.py --state rajasthan --stage plots              # both plot modules
python -m src.plots.make_plots_phases [--refresh] [--only name1,name2] [--jobs N]
```

Output, same layout as the legacy set:
- `results/plots/static/<name>.png` - flat image for reports and slides (tracked in git).
- `results/plots/interactive/<name>.html` - zoom, hover, legend toggles; self-contained (git-ignored, about 4.7 MB each).
- `results/plots_data/` - cached simulation and refit results behind six figures. `--refresh` rebuilds them (about 10 min).

The nine earlier figures (`results/plots/fig1..fig9_*.png`, from `make_plots_results.py`) are separate and unchanged.
The walkthroughs of the legacy set (`00_INDEX.md`, `02..08_*_plots.md`) were deleted on 2026-10-28 with the rest of the legacy docs; they are in git history.

Held-out years: no figure simulates them. Held-out numbers are read from `results/g10_heldout_results.csv`, produced by the single permitted run.

## Viva captions (one line each phase)

- **Phase 2:** "the current space is almost entirely buildable; the only blocked region is a radial-packing pocket, and hydraulics are negligible."
- **Phase 3:** "the multi-node tank shows daily heating and cooling, the PCM melts and refreezes on 295 of 365 days, and the water plateau sits at the 72 C shield limit."
- **Phase 4:** "the multi-node model conserves energy to rounding error; sensitivities have the right sign; flow acts only through a model artefact."
- **Phase 5:** "the Sobol DOE fills the box evenly, and most outcomes are gains over the water-only tank."
- **Phase 6:** "surrogates rank designs well within a pair (Spearman 0.93-0.99) even where the absolute error is large."
- **Phase 7:** "the optimiser reaches the dense-grid front; 96-100 % of the simulated designs stay under the 65 C limit."
- **Phase 8:** "the gain survives all ten weather years; the extra overheating hours do too, and are reported."
