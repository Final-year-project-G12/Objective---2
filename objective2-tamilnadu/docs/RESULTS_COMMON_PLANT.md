**How to read the two seven-criteria tables (sections 9 and 10).** Both describe the SAME seven designs: they are defined once, in `ansys/criteria_common/scenarios.py`, and the MATLAB export (`scripts/c20_m1_export.py`) reads that file. (Before 2026-11-24 criterion 3 differed: a real funnel finalist of 132 capsules here and a rule-built 77 % design of 108 capsules in M1.) They differ in what they MEASURE: the section-9 table is the node model on the select years 2020-21 at dt 150 s (`g_window`), the section-10 table is one select year (2020) at dt 37.5 s (`paired_gain_window_kWh`). So the gains differ by a few kWh, and designs 0 and 1 can swap order between the tables. That is expected: they differ only in flow, their gap is far inside the repeatability band (21-30 kWh), and the funnel declares them tied and breaks the tie on overheating. Criterion 3 (132 capsules) is the weakest of the six funnel finalists, so it is within about 2 kWh of the optimum; the clearly weaker designs are 4, 5 and 6.

## 11. Limits stated

* Simulator results only: no CFD validation, no experiment, no hardware.
* The O1 PCMs (54-58 C melting) gave about zero gain at 100 L/day; a 45 C screen found lower-melting PCMs better, not adopted by decision.
* 175 L/day on a 100 L tank is still below IS 12976's 1.5-2x storage guidance.
* PCM tanks always have more overheat-guard hours than the plain tank.
* Regime-0 medoid is one of 24 tied points; the three regimes use one medoid weather point each.
* The band definition (core vs full) was fixed after a smoke test; both are reported.
* 2024-2025 carry prior project exposure; 2006-2010 weather was not GHI-audited against the plant outcomes before the test.
* Parameters F_R tau-alpha and F_R U_L are the legacy values, not re-fitted for the 2.0 m2 absorber basis.
